MyCCAngel · AI Healthcare Product
MyCCAngel is a digital platform designed specifically for people living with Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD), a chronic condition that can affect daily life through physical discomfort, dietary restrictions, mental health challenges and frequent urgent need for restroom access.

The product aims to build a global support community where patients can connect, share experiences and support one another, while also providing practical tools to help people manage everyday challenges associated with IBD.

My work brings together product discovery, healthcare, responsible AI, user experience, product strategy and commercialization considerations.
