MyCCAngel

AI-powered healthcare community platform designed for people living with Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD). From product strategy and AI feature design to UX direction and data architecture, this project demonstrates my end-to-end product management approach.

View Case Study

Overview

MyCCAngel was created to improve the experience of people living with IBD by providing a trusted, AI-powered community platform. The platform combines secure patient verification, personalized recommendations, community engagement, and healthcare-focused features into one ecosystem.

🎯 Goal

Create a trusted digital community where verified patients can safely connect, access resources, and receive personalized recommendations.

👥 Users

People diagnosed with Crohn's Disease or Ulcerative Colitis seeking reliable support and healthcare resources.

🛠 My Role

Product Strategy • AI Product Lead • UX Direction • Database Design • AI Feature Development • Stakeholder Collaboration

AI Features

🔬 Medical Report Verification

Developed an SVM-based machine learning model that classifies uploaded medical reports as IBD or Non-IBD with approximately 91% accuracy, helping verify patient eligibility.

🎯 Recommendation Engine

Designed a recommendation system using 20+ onboarding signals, including diagnosis type, symptom severity, treatments, and lifestyle impacts to personalize each user's experience.

🗄 Product Data Model

Designed a scalable PostgreSQL relational database supporting verification workflows, consent management, user profiles, community content, and audit history.

📱 Product Design

Defined user journeys, product requirements, and collaborated with designers to build a modern healthcare experience from onboarding through community engagement.
